# Mission // #002

## Chasing Echoes

*“Please, do not touch the merchandise.”*  


# Goals

- Find a way to meet with los Voladores.
- Determine if they have the means to track the unkown signal.
- Make a deal for the equipment needed to do so.

# Stakes

- Establishing a breakthrough in the Omninet Relay Outpost investigation.
- Maintaining the relationship between Union and los Voladores.
- Furthering the knowledge of their culture and technological capabilities and advancing our own.

---
> Listen up, please. Los Voladores have always been a blindspot for Union, and for good reason. After SecComm's attempt at bringing them under hegemony scared them off, the nomads have been very slow to reenter Union space. Normally, we'd be hesitant to test this careful truce, but we've got good reason this time; there's been a new discovery in the Relay Outpost investigation. The scan data we salvaged from the Thunderdome matched up with what we picked up at R10-981—they're one and the same, and that signal blackout they used is some kind of blinktech unlike anything we're familiar with. Unfortunately, we lack the means to trace that kind of signature, but that's where los Voladores come in. They sell all sorts of advance and unknown technology, and we've gotten a tip-off that there's a Voladores trade ship coming into harbor at a local station that just might have the kind of equipment we're looking for. This'll likely require some out-of-chassis diplomacy, which Yang is prepared to assist with, but this station doesn't have the best reputation, so be prepared for anything. Suit up, rangers.

> Captain Bravilles
