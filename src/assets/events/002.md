“Thank you for gathering before me today.” Captain Yi-Fan Bravilles turns from the endless sea of stars beyond the glass to face you, hands clasped behind her back and expression schooled into her usual steely stare. It’s not the first time you’ve been on the bridge of the Silver Lining, but it’s the first time you’ve seen the entire crew brought together like this. A few faces stick out in the crowd. Yang is typing away at a PDA behind the captain, though their eyes flit up to scan the crowd every now and then, even giving you a lazy wave when they spot you. Percy Pontiff is still half-dressed in an EVA suit, the upper half tied around his waist as he fiddles with a repair tool. Doctor Daramy is almost bouncing in place with anticipation, locked in discussion with another researcher, though she startles to a stop as the captain begins speaking.

“As most of you are aware, our team has, for some time, been involved in the investigation into the decomissioning and subsequent disappearance of Omninet Relay Outpost R10-981, formerly of the Sharbāt system. Now that we’ve established both the presence of anomalous blink activity and the means by which to track it, Union Naval forces have agreed to lend us a hand and provide access to previously-sealed naval intelligence regarding similar activity.”

This sends a ripple of murmuring though the room, which Bravilles silences with a raise of her gloved hand. “I know. I’m not happy that this was something already known to Union either. But understand that what I’m about to relay was considered pertinent to ongoing conflicts taking place in Boundary Garden, which is both spatially very distant and outside our usual purview as researchers, and thus not worth risking a potential leak of sensitive Naval intelligence. This is not to be shared.” She lets the words sit heavy in the air for a few moments before continuing.

“Those of you familiar with the history of the Third Commission may be aware of the First Interest War, in which the Federal Karrakin Monarchy came into conflict with the newly-formed Harrison Armory. Cradle largely stayed out of the conflict, with the dust of the fall of SecComm still settling, but still had resources in the region capable of monitoring the war. That was the first time the Karrakin-Armory Sustained Blinkfield Anomaly was observed, or KASBA for short. Obviously, the initial suspicion was that it was some sort of experimental technology either Karrakis or, more likely, the Armory had cooked up and was testing on the battlefield.

“However, as our intelligence operations started reporting in, it became clear that this wasn't the case. Leadership from both sides suspected it was a weapon of some kind from the other. The eye-witness reports only solidified this: stories of a massive blot against the stars, only found within the clouds of wreckage left over after engagements between capital fleets. Something that could have been chalked up to sensors failing to penetrate complex debris fields, if not for its unique Blinkfield signature that disrupted all local signals. Some reports even supposedly observed the field enveloping wrecks and leaving nothing behind after moving on.

“Cradle was too busy sorting themselves out and working towards ending the Interest War to investigate thoroughly, and the trail went cold around the same time the fighting stopped. There were still scattered reports of shadows surrounding wrecks, often attributed by cosmopolitans to a ‘space kraken’ sinking vessels, but most failed to be conclusively linked to the KASBA. However, the anomaly has begun to show itself once more around the aftermath of naval engagements in Boundary Garden, and of course with our first glimpse inside the field thanks to our Armored Expeditionary Personnel, its nature as a threat can no longer be denied.”

Every pair of eyes in the room turns to you, the captain’s included. “I understand that this level of secrecy is unusual for our team. If you have any further questions, I’ll answer them to the best of my abilities. Otherwise, we’ve got another few weeks before the two corvettes from the local Naval outpost arrive and we’re cleared to engage.”
